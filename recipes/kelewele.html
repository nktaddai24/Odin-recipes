Description

Kelewele is one of Ghana's most beloved street foods, made from ripe plantains seasoned with aromatic spices and fried until golden brown. The combination of sweet plantains, fresh ginger, and spicy peppers creates a delicious balance of flavors that makes this dish irresistible. Popular as a snack, appetizer, or side dish, Kelewele is commonly enjoyed in the evenings and is often served with roasted groundnuts (peanuts) for an authentic Ghanaian experience.

The beauty of Kelewele lies in its simplicity and rich taste. The crispy exterior and soft, sweet interior make every bite satisfying, while the spices add warmth and depth. Whether served at family gatherings, parties, or enjoyed as a quick snack, Kelewele is a true representation of Ghana's vibrant food culture and is guaranteed to impress anyone who tastes it.

Ingredients

  • Ripe palntains
  • Fresh grated ginger
  • garlic minced
  • Chilli powder
  • Ground cloves
  • Nutmeg
  • Salt for taste
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Steps

  3. Add the plantains to the bowl and mix thoroughly until each piece is evenly coated with the spices.
  4. Allow the seasoned plantains to marinate for 15 to 20 minutes to absorb the flavors.
  5. Heat vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
