Description

This salad is not only refreshing but also versatile—you can adjust the protein by swapping tuna for chicken or adding more beans for a vegetarian option. The layering technique makes it visually appealing, and the creamy dressing balances the crisp vegetables beautifully. It’s a dish that impresses both in taste and presentation, perfect for festive occasions or a simple family meal.

Ingredients

  • lettuce, washed and shredded
  • medium tomatoes, sliced
  • carrots, grated
  • hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • baked beans
  • mustard
  • vinegar or lemon juice
  • salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare vegetables
    Wash and slice the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, and grate the carrots.
  2. Boil eggs until firm, peel, and slice them.
  3. In a large bowl, layer lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, carrots, baked beans, and tuna.
  4. Place sliced eggs on top for garnish.
  5. Combine mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar or lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
  6. Serve
    Drizzle dressing over the salad before serving, or serve it on the side.
